# TimeTiles Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 2026 (v2.0.0)**

## Summary

TimeTiles does **not** collect, send or share any personal data. Everything happens inside your browser.

## How it works

- All times are worked out locally using your browser's built-in time zone data
- No external servers or APIs are contacted, and no data leaves your device
- Your own time zone is read from your browser to show "You are here". It is not stored or sent anywhere
- Location (GPS) is **not** used. This permission was removed in v2.0.0

## What is stored

Only your settings, saved with Chrome's own storage (synced to your Chrome profile if Chrome sync is on):

- 12 hour or 24 hour clock choice
- The places you add (city name and time zone)

You can remove places at any time with the × button, or remove the extension to delete everything.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| `storage` | Saves your clock choice and your places |
| `sidePanel` | Shows TimeTiles as a side panel |
| `clipboardWrite` | Lets the Copy for Slack button copy times to your clipboard |
